Hloov kho zaum kawg: 31 Lub Kaum Hli 2026

Koj thov tau kom peb rho koj tus account Daim Av Hlub, thiab cov ntaub ntawv nrog nws, txhua lub sij hawm.

Thov li cas

  1. Xa xov rau peb (saib "Tiv tauj peb" hauv qab no) hais tias "Rho kuv tus account", nrog koj lub npe thiab email los yog xov tooj ntawm koj tus account, los yog tus account Facebook los yog Google uas koj siv nkag.
  2. Peb yuav thov kom koj lees tias yog koj tiag, kom tsis muaj lwm tus rho tau koj tus account.
  3. Peb rho tawm hauv 30 hnub thiab qhia koj thaum tiav lawm.

Yam peb rho tawm

Koj tus account, kev txuas rau koj tus account Facebook los yog Google, koj qhov teeb kev ceeb toom, koj cov lus sau, nyiam thiab kev teb rau kev tshwm sim, thiab yam koj qhia (cov post, kev tshwm sim, dab neeg, khoom muag thiab duab). Yog koj xav tau, peb khaws tau yam koj qhia tseg yam tsis muaj koj lub npe.

Yam peb khaws tseg

Cov ntaub ntawv xaj thiab kev them nyiaj, ntev npaum li peb xav tau rau peb cov nyiaj txiag thiab txoj cai; peb muab koj tus account tshem tawm ntawm lawv. Cov xov twb xa rau koj lawm (email, WhatsApp) rub rov qab tsis tau.

Nkag nrog Facebook los yog Google?

  • Google: myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Daim Av Hlub → Delete all connections.
  • Tshem nws tawm ntawd yuav tsis pub Facebook los yog Google qhia rau peb ntxiv lawm. Kom rho yam peb twb muaj lawm, thov peb raws li saum toj no.
