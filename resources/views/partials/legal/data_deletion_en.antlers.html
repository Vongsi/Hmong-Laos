You can ask us to delete your Daim Av Hlub account, and the data that goes with it, at any time.

How to ask

  1. Send us a message (see "Contact us" below) saying "Delete my account", with your name and the email or phone number of your account, or the Facebook or Google account you sign in with.
  2. We may ask you to confirm it is you, so nobody else can delete your account.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What we delete

Your account and profile, the link to your Facebook or Google account, your notification settings, your comments, likes and replies to events, and what you shared (posts, events, stories, products and photos). If you prefer, we can keep what you shared without your name.

What we keep

Records of orders and payments, for as long as we need them for our accounts and the law; we take your account off them. Messages already sent to you (email, WhatsApp) can't be taken back.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

  • To stop using it with us: {{ trans key="site.nav.account" }} → {{ trans key="site.auth.sign_in_methods" }} → {{ trans key="site.auth.disconnect" }}.
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Daim Av Hlub → Remove.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Daim Av Hlub → Delete all connections.
  • Removing it there stops Facebook or Google sharing with us. To delete what we already hold, ask us as above.
