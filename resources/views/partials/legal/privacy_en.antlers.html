Daim Av Hlub is a website for the Hmong community: news, events, stories, members and a shop. This page explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can see, change or delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email, and your password, which we store encrypted (we can't read it). If you join or sign in with Facebook or Google, we get only your name, your email (if they have one) and an account number from them, not your friends, posts or photos.
  • What you add to your profile: mobile number, province, district or village, clan, the language you want messages in, and whether to appear on the Members page. Members who sell products also give the bank account we pay them to.
  • What you share: posts, events, stories, products, photos, comments, likes and replies to events.
  • Orders: your name, phone, email (optional), delivery address, note, what you bought, how you pay, the payment receipts you upload, and the payment messages our bank sends us.
  • Messages to you: if you allow notifications, an address to notify that device. When you order, we send updates about your order on WhatsApp to the phone number you give, and keep a copy of each message.
  • Technical: cookies that keep you signed in and remember your cart, and when you were last active (shown on the Members page and to the team). Your device remembers your recent orders and whether you chose light or dark colours; that stays on your device. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we use it

  • to run your account and show what you share;
  • to take, deliver and confirm orders, check payments, and pay members for the products they sell;
  • to send the updates and notifications you asked for;
  • to check what is shared and keep the community safe.

Who else sees it

We never sell your data. We share only what each service needs:

  • Facebook (Meta) and Google, only if you sign in with them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the order updates we send to your phone.
  • Anthropic (the company behind Claude, an AI) reads the payment receipts you upload, so we can check them against your order faster. It is used only to read the receipt for us.
  • Banks (BCEL One) for QR payments, and delivery companies (such as Anousith Express), which get your name, phone and address to deliver your order.
  • The companies that host our website (servers in Singapore) and send our emails.
  • Everyone: what you share appears on the site with your name once the team has checked it, and the team may also post it on our Facebook Page. Your name and province appear on the Members page only if you choose. Your phone, clan, district and orders are seen only by you and the team.

How long we keep it

Your account and profile stay until you delete your account. Orders, payments and the messages about them are kept as long as we need them for our accounts and the law, then deleted.

Your choices

  • See and change your details on {{ trans key="site.nav.account" }}, and connect or disconnect Facebook and Google there.
  • Turn notifications off in your browser or phone settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it: see {{ trans key="site.legal.data_deletion" }}.

Children

Children under 13 should join only with a parent's permission.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
