Daim Av Hlub is a website for the Hmong community: news, events, stories, members and a shop. This page explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can see, change or delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email, and your password, which we store encrypted (we can't read it). If you join or sign in with Facebook or Google, we get only your name, your email (if they have one) and an account number from them, not your friends, posts or photos. If you sign in with WhatsApp, we keep your WhatsApp number to send you sign-in codes there; the codes themselves are never kept.
  • What you add to your profile: profile photo, mobile number, country, province, district or village (or city), clan, the language you want messages in, and whether to appear on the Members page. Members who sell products also give the bank account we pay them to.
  • What you share: posts, events, stories, products, photos, comments, likes and replies to events.
  • Private messages: the messages and photos you send other members on the Messages page, the members you block and the reports you make, including in the groups you make or join. The text is stored encrypted, and the photos are private files. When you edit a message we keep only the new text; when you delete one for everyone, its text and photo are deleted.
  • Calls: when you call another member, we keep who called whom, when and for how long, as a note in your chat; nothing else. Calls go straight between your two phones or computers, encrypted. When your networks can't connect directly, they pass through our server, still encrypted, so nobody (not even us) can listen. Calls are never recorded.
  • Orders: your name, phone, email (optional), delivery address, note, what you bought, how you pay, the payment receipts you upload, and the payment messages our bank sends us.
  • Messages to you: if you allow notifications, an address to notify that device. When you order, we send updates about your order on WhatsApp to the phone number you give, and keep a copy of each message.
  • Technical: cookies that keep you signed in and remember your cart, and when you were last active (shown on the Members page and to the team). Your device remembers your recent orders and whether you chose light or dark colours; that stays on your device. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we use it

  • to run your account and show what you share;
  • to take, deliver and confirm orders, check payments, and pay members for the products they sell;
  • to send the updates and notifications you asked for;
  • to check what is shared and keep the community safe.

Who else sees it

We never sell your data. We share only what each service needs:

  • Facebook (Meta) and Google, only if you sign in with them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the order updates we send to your phone.
  • Anthropic (the company behind Claude, an AI) reads the payment receipts you upload, so we can check them against your order faster. It is used only to read the receipt for us.
  • Banks (BCEL One) for QR payments, and delivery companies (such as Anousith Express), which get your name, phone and address to deliver your order.
  • The companies that host our website (servers in Singapore) and send our emails.
  • Google (Google Drive) keeps our nightly backups. We encrypt them first, so Google cannot read them.
  • Your messages are seen only by you and the members of that chat or group (people who join a group later don't see what was written before). If someone reports a chat or a group, the team sees its latest messages (up to 20) to decide what to do. A member you write to for the first time sees your message as a request, and nobody is put in a group without accepting.
  • Everyone: what you share appears on the site with your name once the team has checked it, and the team may also post it on our Facebook Page. Signed-in members see your name, photo, province (or country, outside Laos) and when you're online on the Members page; you can hide yourself there or on My account. Your phone, clan, district and orders are seen only by you and the team.

How long we keep it

Your account and profile stay until you delete your account. Orders, payments and the messages about them are kept as long as we need them for our accounts and the law, then deleted. Messages stay until everyone in the chat has deleted it. When you delete your account, your chats with one member are deleted, and you leave your groups (your messages and photos there are deleted too).

Your choices

  • See and change your details on {{ trans key="site.nav.account" }}, and connect or disconnect Facebook and Google there.
  • Turn notifications off in your browser or phone settings.
  • On the Messages page, edit your messages or delete them for everyone (for 24 hours), leave a group, block or report a member, report a group, or delete a chat; on {{ trans key="site.nav.account" }}, turn off new message requests.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it: see {{ trans key="site.legal.data_deletion" }}.
